ADM extension is installed

It sends your browser's downloads to the ADM app.

1. ADM must be running

This extension is only a bridge: the downloading is done by the ADM app on your computer. If ADM is not running, your browser keeps downloading files as usual, so nothing is lost.

2. Logged-in downloads optional

Some downloads only work when you are signed in to the site. To make those work, ADM needs the same cookies your browser would send. This is off until you allow it, and the cookies go only to ADM on this computer.

3. Find videos on pages optional

With browser monitoring on, the toolbar icon lists the videos and audio a page loads, each with a download button. It reads each response's type and size, and the headers of requests that can become downloads, so ADM can repeat them exactly. Nothing leaves your computer.

4. Using it

  • Start a download in the browser as usual: it opens in ADM instead.
  • Right-click a link, video or audio and choose Download with ADM.
  • Click the toolbar icon to see videos found on the page, or to grab all the links on it.

Nothing is sent to any server on the internet by this extension. See the privacy policy in the store listing.
